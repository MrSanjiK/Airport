# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + Three.js + GSAP, built as a static site (user-confirmed). Deployable to any static host (Vercel / Netlify).

## Users

- **Primary:** marketing directors, brand managers and media buyers at companies advertising in Uzbekistan — banks and financial services, telecom, auto dealers, real-estate developers, airlines and tour operators, insurance, hotels, premium and FMCG brands — plus international brands entering the market and the advertising agencies that buy on their behalf.
- **Situation:** planning a campaign and choosing indoor digital (DOOH) placements; comparing audience, screen formats, zones, durations and monthly cost before requesting a media plan.
- **Job:** understand which network reaches which audience, see the real inventory and price, assemble a plan, and send an inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Media Port sells advertising placements on indoor screens and lightboxes across Uzbekistan:

1. **Tashkent International Airport (Islam Karimov)** — large-format LED screens by zone, a 200-monitor TV network, and backlit lightboxes.
2. **Korzinka supermarkets** — checkout-zone monitors plus in-store audio announcements, nationwide.
3. **HoReCa** — monitors with hall speakers in 75+ restaurants and cafes in Tashkent.
4. **Regions** — 100+ venues (supermarkets, cafes, restaurants) in 11 cities and regions.

Success = a qualified visitor builds or requests a media plan (inquiry submitted, or a call / Telegram / WhatsApp contact).

## Positioning

One operator, one buy, from the country's most premium audience to its widest one: the airport's 18 million visits a year (decision-makers, investors, travelers, a captive audience that cannot switch channels) together with Korzinka's 900,000 daily receipts, 75+ restaurants and 100+ regional venues. Media Port is the indoor-media division of the Orient Advertising holding (100+ specialists), built specifically to operate at Tashkent International Airport.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate with commercial-offer decks (the PDFs in the workspace): audience stats, photos of real screens in place, per-zone spec tables (screen count, physical size, resolution, spot length, plays per day, hours), and a 3 / 6 / 12-month price ladder (monthly price, sum, excluding VAT). Typical next step: request a media plan, then call or message.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the screen in place.** Every network is proven by its real location, audience number and spec, not by adjectives.
2. **Price is part of the pitch.** Inventory and the 3 / 6 / 12 ladder are visible and calculable; the path from interest to media plan is short.
3. **Premium audience, national reach.** The airport carries prestige; Korzinka, HoReCa and Regions carry scale. The story connects both under one buy.
4. **Every number has a source.** Use deck figures as stated; never round up or invent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Nine languages including right-to-left Arabic; layouts must mirror correctly for RTL and handle CJK and Korean scripts. Heavy 3D and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and keep content readable without WebGL.
